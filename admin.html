How to read this

Selection gap = mean peer score of a person's submitted ideas minus that of their non-submitted ideas. Positive means people submitted their better ideas. It needs peer evaluations of non-submitted ideas (PEER_POOL = 'all' or external rating).
Self–peer ρ = within-person Spearman correlation between self-assessment (rank in condition 2, slider in condition 3) and peer score, averaged over participants with ≥3 peer-evaluated ideas.
Peer score = (set size − rank) / (set size − 1), so rankings from sets of different size are comparable.
